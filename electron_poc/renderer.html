Rarity Drop Weights

Control drop-weight multipliers from GameState.RarityState. 100% is vanilla weight, 50% is half weight, and 0% removes that rarity from the drop pool.

Adjust weights, then Apply. Saved presets only change the visible sliders until you apply them.
