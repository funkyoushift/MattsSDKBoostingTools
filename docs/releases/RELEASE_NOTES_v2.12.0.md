### What's new

- A redesigned desktop workspace groups tools by task: Character & Boosting, Items & Inventory, Spawning & Waves, Movement & World, Party & Combat, Editors & Advanced, and Shortcuts & Settings.
- Crowded pages now have focused sections, including Levels & XP, Currency, Unlocks & Capacity, and Challenges & UVH. Existing game actions keep their original behavior.
- Layouts adapt to narrow, half-screen, maximized, and large windows. Navigation becomes a drawer in narrow windows, controls wrap, and the actor browser and spawn settings stack without overlapping.
- Actor names and internal IDs are easier to distinguish. Search (Ctrl+K) opens the relevant section.
- Use **Classic layout** in the header to return to the previous interface. Saved classic panel layouts are retained.

### Finding player targets

In Boosting, expand **Choose players & spawn location** above the action controls. Choose Local, All Players, Other Players, or Named Player; Refresh Status loads the party roster.

### Downloads

Choose **MSBT-Installer-v2.12.0.exe**, or extract **MSBT-Portable-v2.12.0-win-x64.zip** for the portable app. The matching SDK mod is bundled and also available separately.

The Android controller remains version 1.1.0. Its existing APKs are included so mobile downloads continue to work.

Requires oak2-mod-manager v0.3. Close Borderlands 4 before installing an SDK mod update.

### Validation

Offline checks cover 468 page/section and window-size combinations (360px through 4K), larger text, populated actor lists, existing action dispatch, classic walkthroughs, window persistence, and Quick Menu/bridge regressions. Live multiplayer gameplay was not exercised for this UI release.
