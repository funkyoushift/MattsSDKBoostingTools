StreamerChaos — verified working effects (from TwitchInteractionProbe)

Hotkeys (rebind in oak2 / SDK options UI):
  NumPad5  Launch player (AddImpulse Z+5000)
  NumPad6  Drop backpack to ground (SpillOut)
  NumPad7  Delete backpack (EmptyContainer)
  NumPad8  FFYL (StartDownState False)
  NumPad9  Kill (StartDownState True)
  NumPad4  Invert look (8s, then restore)
  NumPad1  Lock look (5s)
  NumPad2  Lock move (5s)
  NumPad3  Lock look+move (5s)
  NumPad0  Unlock look/move now

Console:
  sc_help
  sc_launch [z]
  sc_drop_backpack
  sc_empty_backpack
  sc_kill
  sc_ffyl
  sc_invert [seconds]
  sc_lock_look [seconds]
  sc_lock_move [seconds]
  sc_lock_both [seconds]
  sc_unlock

Notes:
  - Hotkeys always affect LOCAL player.
  - This mod is the Vault Surge / streamer-app feed. MSBT does NOT need it
    (same effects are built into MSBT streamer_chaos.py + chaos_* bridge actions).
  - Drop = whole backpack SpillOut. Equipped-only drop is NOT included.
  - Folder mods do not hot-reload — full game restart after install/edit.
  - If StreamerChaos + MSBT are both loaded, NumPad defaults can double-fire.
